# Connectors

This plugin works standalone from screenshots, code, descriptions, research files, and design-system documentation.

## Included connector

| Category | Server | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Design | Figma MCP | Read design context, inspect components and variables, create/update supported Figma content, and improve design-to-code or code-to-design workflows. |

The included remote endpoint is `https://mcp.figma.com/mcp`.

Additional product analytics, user-feedback, project-tracking, and knowledge-base connectors can strengthen research synthesis and design decisions, but are not required by the plugin.
